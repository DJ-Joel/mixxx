Beat check models (Auto DJ 2.0 plus Video Mixing)

mel_spectrogram.onnx  - log-mel front end of "Beat This!"
beat_this_small.onnx  - "Beat This!" small beat and downbeat model
(beat_this.onnx, the full model, is not in this folder: it goes in the
stems engine folder, model/beat_this.onnx.)

"Beat This!" by Francesco Foscarin, Jan Schlüter and Gerhard Widmer,
"Beat this! Accurate beat tracking without DBN postprocessing", ISMIR 2024,
https://github.com/CPJKU/beat_this . The ONNX files are the conversions
from https://github.com/danigb/beat-this-rs (models/ and its "model-large"
release; beat_this.onnx sha256
5f810debe53459b559127fb55bbad40035bb47cc567b20e501670f968c770f02).
